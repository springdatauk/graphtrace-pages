GraphTrace Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

Your project data

Spreadsheets, names, mappings, graphs and change reports are processed and stored on your device. We do not receive or upload this content. Relate by Neo4j runs inside the app and does not connect to a database service. GraphTrace has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Files and sharing

The app reads files you select and stores an imported copy in its private application storage. Camera access is optional. GraphTrace uses it when you take a photo for a graph item. The reduced image is stored locally with that project. Project files include the reduced copies of photos you attach. PNG and PDF graph exports include photos when you select Include photos. Export creates a file wherever you choose. Anyone you share that file with can read its content. Your device backup or chosen file provider may store copies under that provider’s terms. GraphTrace does not automatically synchronise projects.

Purchases

Apple handles purchases and payments. The app uses signed StoreKit subscription information locally to decide whether Plus is active. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide the developer with sales and financial reports under its own policies.

Retention and deletion

Projects remain until you delete them using Project actions → Delete project. Original spreadsheets, exported copies and device backups are separate: delete those using their respective apps or providers. Uninstalling the app on iOS removes its local container; on Mac, application data may remain after deleting the app.

Contact and rights

For privacy enquiries contact hello@springdata.uk. If you email us, we use your address and message to answer your request, and retain correspondence only as needed to respond and meet legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal information we hold. We cannot retrieve project data that remains solely on your device. Where applicable, you may raise a concern with your local data protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children and changes

GraphTrace is a productivity tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. We may update this policy when the app changes; the effective date identifies the current version.

This website

These pages use no cookies, analytics, external fonts or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website hosting is separate from the app’s local project storage.
